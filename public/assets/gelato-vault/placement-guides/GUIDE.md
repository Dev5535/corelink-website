# CoreLink Gelato Placement Guide

## General Rules
- **Margins:** Maintain a 1.5cm safe zone from all seams.
- **DPI:** All print files must be 300 DPI.
- **Color Profile:** sRGB (Gelato converts to CMYK automatically).

## 1. Hoodie (All-Over Print)
- **Base:** `nebula_corelink_master_v1.png` tiled seamlessly.
- **Logo:** `neon_angel_logo_v1.png`
  - **Front:** Left Chest, 8cm width, 15cm down from shoulder seam.
  - **Back:** Center, 25cm width, 10cm down from collar.
- **Cuffs/Hem:** Solid black or dark blue (no nebula) for contrast.

## 2. T-Shirt (Standard)
- **Base:** Black or Dark Navy garment.
- **Print Area:** Front Center.
- **Graphic:** `neon_angel_logo_v1.png`
  - **Placement:** Chest Center.
  - **Size:** 25cm width.

## 3. Joggers (All-Over Print)
- **Base:** `nebula_corelink_master_v1.png` tiled.
- **Side Stripe:** Optional solid black stripe (5cm width) down outer legs.
- **Logo:** Small (5cm) on right hip pocket area.

## 4. Duvet Cover & Pillow
- **Base:** `nebula_corelink_master_v1.png` (Adult) or `nebula_corelink_soft_v1.png` (Kids).
- **Scale:** Pattern scale 100% (do not stretch).
- **Logo:** None on bedding (keep it abstract).

## 5. Footwear (Canvas Shoes)
- **Base:** `nebula_corelink_wrap_v1.png`.
- **Tongue:** Solid black.
- **Laces:** Cyan or Black.
